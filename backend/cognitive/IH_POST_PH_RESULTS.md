**What Growl does:**
Small business owners (especially restaurants) spend 3+ hours/week doing competitor research manually. Growl automates it: 3C analysis, STP segmentation, and competitor mapping in ~2 minutes. Built because I was doing this for my own burger shop and it was eating my Sundays.

**What I learned from the launch:**

1. **The first 2 hours matter most.** PH's algorithm weights early engagement heavily. If you don't have supporters ready to upvote in the first 2 hours, you start the day behind.

2. **The maker comment is your real pitch.** Don't waste it on generic "excited to launch" copy. I wrote about a real problem I had — 3 hours/week of manual research — and asked a genuine question at the end. That's what gets replies.

3. **Launching from Japan is hard.** The 12:01am PDT launch time is 4pm JST, which is actually good for me. But my network is mostly Japanese, and PH is English-first. Without an English-speaking audience warmed up, cold launches are tough.

---

**What I'm doing next:**

- Replying to every PH comment within the hour
- Sharing the PH link on Bluesky (@kanagawatable)
- Starting Cold DMs to restaurant owners on Twitter this week

If you're a restaurant owner or small business owner who's curious about Growl, I'd genuinely love your feedback. Not selling — just want to know if the 3C/STP output is actually useful for you.

What does your competitor research process look like right now?
