本文:
I run a small restaurant in Japan. I also build AI tools on the side.

Last week I got tired of not knowing where to start with marketing. So I built a free tool in 30 minutes that diagnoses your SNS marketing in 5 questions.

It asks: what industry, posting frequency, biggest struggle, review management, 3-month goal. Then Groq AI gives you an A–E score + one specific action you can do today in 5 minutes.

Every result page has a "Share your score" button. Shamelessly borrowed from the personality-quiz-funnel playbook.

Try it (free, no signup):
https://growl-app.vercel.app/diagnosis

Built with: Next.js + Groq (llama-3.3-70b). ~30 minutes from idea to deployed.
Numbers so far: 0 users, $0 revenue. Just launched today.
