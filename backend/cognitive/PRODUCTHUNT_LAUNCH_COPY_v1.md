## FIRST-HOUR COMMENT REPLIES

**Q: "How is this different from just asking ChatGPT?"**
> ChatGPT will hallucinate competitor names and market positions — it has no live data. Growl uses Tavily to pull real, current competitor info, then structures it into a 3C framework that's actually actionable. Try the same question in both and compare the output quality.

**Q: "Does this work for [non-restaurant industry, e.g. salon / contractor]?"**
> Yes — I built it for restaurants originally but the 3C framework applies to any local business. Salons, contractors, EC stores, clinics. If you have competitors and customers, Growl can map them. Would love to hear how it works for your specific industry.

**Q: "What happens after the free trial?"**
> Standard plan is ¥3,000/month (~$20) — covers unlimited analyses for one business. Pro is ¥8,000/month for multiple locations or businesses. After the trial you get a straight pricing page, no tricks. Happy to answer anything specific about what's included.
