### The identity layer (what makes this a clone, not a bot)

Three files define who Sage is:

**identity.json** — your niche, tone, audience, brand name. Change these and every piece of content shifts immediately.

**SOUL.md** — your values, what you will and won't say, your communication style. The personality layer.

**HEARTBEAT.md** — your autonomous schedule. What posts at 9am, what runs weekly. You set it once. Sage runs it forever.
