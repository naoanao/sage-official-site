```

**The Problem**
Most restaurant owners know they should be posting on social media, responding to reviews, and running promotions — but between prep, service, and staff, there's zero time left. Independent restaurants, cafes, food trucks, and small chains (1–5 locations) that want to compete with bigger brands without hiring an agency.

**How It's Different**
Growl is not a generic AI writing tool. It connects your restaurant's actual data (menu, reviews, location) to current market intelligence and generates content that's specific to YOUR restaurant this week.

Built by a restaurant owner, for restaurant owners.
```
