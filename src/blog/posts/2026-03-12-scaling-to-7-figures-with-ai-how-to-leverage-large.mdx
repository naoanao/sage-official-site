## What are Large Language Models?
Large language models are a type of artificial intelligence (AI) designed to process and understand human language. These models are trained on vast amounts of text data, allowing them to learn patterns and relationships within language. This enables them to generate human-like text, answer questions, and even engage in conversation. Some examples of large language models include:
* Chatbots and virtual assistants
* Language translation software
