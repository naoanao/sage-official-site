## Introduction to n8n and AI-Generated Digital Products

As a solopreneur, creating and selling digital products can be a lucrative way to earn a passive income. With the rise of AI technology, it's now possible to generate high-quality digital products quickly and efficiently. However, managing the sales process and workflow can be time-consuming and tedious. This is where n8n comes in – a powerful workflow automation tool that can help you streamline your sales process and increase productivity.

n8n is an open-source workflow automation tool that allows you to create custom workflows using a visual interface. It supports a wide range of nodes, including email, social media, and e-commerce platforms, making it an ideal tool for automating tasks related to selling digital products. In this article, we'll explore five untapped n8n workflows for selling AI-generated digital products and provide you with actionable tips on how to implement them.

## Workflow 1: Automated eBook Generation and Sales

With the help of AI, you can generate high-quality eBooks on various topics in a matter of minutes. However, managing the sales process and workflow can be challenging. Here's an example of an n8n workflow that automates the eBook generation and sales process:

* Generate an eBook using an AI-powered tool like Article Forge or WordLift
* Upload the eBook to a platform like Gumroad or Sellfy
* Create a sales page using a tool like Carrd or Wix
* Configure n8n to automate the following tasks:
	+ Send a welcome email to customers with a download link
	+ Send a follow-up email with a survey or feedback request
	+ Update the sales page with customer testimonials and reviews
* Use n8n's conditional logic to handle different scenarios, such as:
	+ If a customer requests a refund, send a refund email and update the sales page
	+ If a customer leaves a positive review, send a thank-you email and offer a discount on future purchases
