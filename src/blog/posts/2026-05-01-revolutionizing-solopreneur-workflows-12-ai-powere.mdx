## What is Zapier and How Does it Work?

Zapier is an automation tool that connects different web applications, allowing them to work together seamlessly. With Zapier, solopreneurs can create custom automations, known as "Zaps," that automate repetitive tasks, freeing up time for more strategic and creative work. Zapier integrates with over 1,000 web applications, including popular tools like Gmail, Trello, and Slack.
