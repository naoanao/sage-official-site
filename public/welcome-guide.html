Setup walkthrough

Your first revenue pipeline

  1. Open the Sage dashboard
    Go to your-site.pages.dev/dashboard. Enter the email you subscribed with and you'll land directly in the dashboard.
  2. Set your Identity — the most important step
    In the sidebar, open 🎭 Identity. Fill in your Role (e.g. "wellness coach"), Niche (e.g. "burnout recovery for remote workers"), and Tone (e.g. "warm and practical"). Hit Save Identity. From this point on, every piece of content Sage generates is written for your specific audience — not a generic template.
  3. Generate your first content
    Type a topic idea into the Create input. Keep it simple: "5 morning habits for burnout recovery" or "Why remote workers lose focus by 2pm". Hit Generate. In about 90 seconds you'll get a full blog post + 5 social captions + sales copy — all written in your voice.
  4. Review and publish
    Read the output. If the blog post looks good, copy it to your publishing platform (WordPress, Substack, Notion, etc.). The Bluesky caption can be auto-posted — check the ⚡ Automations panel to enable daily auto-posting.
  5. Let Sage run daily
    Once automations are on, Sage generates and posts content every day without you doing anything. Check in once a week to review output and adjust your Identity if your niche or tone shifts.

Pro tip

How to write a great Identity

Example: Fitness coach

Role: "Online fitness coach for busy parents"
Niche: "Quick home workouts and healthy habits for parents with young kids"
Tone: "Friendly, encouraging, realistic — no fluff, no perfect-body pressure"

Example: AI tools creator

Role: "AI tools reviewer and solopreneur educator"
Niche: "Helping freelancers earn more with AI automation tools under $50/month"
Tone: "Direct, skeptical, practical — test everything before recommending"

The more specific you are, the better the output. Generic inputs like "entrepreneur" produce generic content. Specific inputs produce content your audience actually recognizes.
