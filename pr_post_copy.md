### Tagline (60文字以内)
**"3 AI marketing actions every week. Just copy and paste."**

### Intro (300文字)
Japanese small businesses spend hours each week wondering "what should I post?" Growl fixes that.

Tell us your business type (salon, restaurant, EC shop, etc.) and goal. Every week, AI generates 3 ready-to-use content pieces: an Instagram post, a LINE message, and a Google Review reply — all formatted for immediate use.

No login. No credit card. 1 minute to set up. Works in English and Japanese.

### How it's different
- **Real market data**: Sage AI scans Google Trends + Reddit weekly. That signal feeds Growl's generation — content reflects actual current trends, not generic templates.
- **3 channels, 1 plan**: Instagram + LINE + Google Reviews. Each formatted for its platform.
- **¥3,000/mo Standard**: Delivered automatically to your LINE every Monday 8am. No app needed.

### First comment (for PH launch day)
Solo-built this in 6 months. No team. Currently running 2 autonomous Bluesky accounts (1,616 posts total, zero manual posting since Day 1). Growl is the revenue arm of that same stack. Happy to answer any technical questions!
